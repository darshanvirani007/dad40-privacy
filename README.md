# Dad40

Dad40 is a privacy-focused pregnancy companion designed for dads and partners.
---

# About Dad40

Dad40 helps dads and partners understand pregnancy week by week.

The app provides:

- Pregnancy week tracking
- Estimated due-date countdown
- Weekly pregnancy information
- Partner-focused information
- Dad Missions
- Preparation checklist
- Notes
- Moments and milestones
- Local notifications
- Birth mode

Dad40 is designed primarily for adults.

---

# Medical Disclaimer

Dad40 provides general educational and organisational information and is not medical advice.

Pregnancy experiences vary.

For questions about pregnancy or health, users should contact their maternity or healthcare professional.

Users should seek urgent medical help when appropriate.

Dad40 is not:

- a medical device
- a diagnostic tool
- a symptom checker
- an emergency service
- a substitute for professional healthcare

---

# Privacy

Dad40 is designed to keep pregnancy information private.

Information such as:

- estimated due date
- pregnancy week
- notes
- milestones
- checklist information
- Dad Mission progress
- notification settings
- baby-arrival information

is designed to remain locally on the user's Android device.

Dad40 does not require a Dad40 account.

Dad40 does not operate a cloud database containing this pregnancy information.

Dad40 does not intentionally send this pregnancy information to Google AdMob for advertising targeting.

---

# Advertising

Dad40 uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google's advertising technology may process certain technical information such as:

- IP address
- device or advertising identifiers
- app/ad interactions
- diagnostic information

for purposes including:

- advertising
- analytics
- security
- fraud prevention

Dad40 does not intentionally provide a user's:

- due date
- pregnancy week
- pregnancy notes
- milestones
- checklist entries
- birth information

to AdMob as custom advertising targeting information.

---

# Advertising Consent

Dad40 uses Google's consent/privacy messaging technology where required.

Advertising consent and privacy options may depend on the user's country or region.

Users can access applicable advertising privacy choices from inside the app where required.

---

# Google Play Data Safety

The Google Play Data Safety declaration must match the exact production version of Dad40.

Because Dad40 uses Google AdMob, Dad40 must not state that absolutely no data is collected by any SDK.

Google Mobile Ads technology may automatically process data including:

- IP address
- user product interactions
- diagnostic information
- device/account identifiers

The production Data Safety declaration must be reviewed whenever advertising SDKs or other third-party SDKs are changed.

---

# Health Apps Declaration

Dad40 contains pregnancy-related educational content.

The Google Play Health Apps declaration must be completed accurately before release.

Dad40 does not claim to diagnose, treat, prevent, cure, or monitor medical conditions.

# app-ads.txt

Dad40 uses app-ads.txt for AdMob ownership verification.

The production app-ads.txt file is available at:

https://YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME.github.io/app-ads.txt

The publisher ID inside app-ads.txt must exactly match the publisher ID supplied by the AdMob account.

---

# Ads Policy

Dad40 advertisements should not:

- imitate app controls
- cause accidental clicks
- cover navigation
- interrupt sensitive pregnancy-loss flows
- interrupt medical disclaimers
- appear beside destructive actions
- encourage users to click advertisements

Dad40 must never ask users to click advertisements to support the app.

---

# Sensitive Information

Dad40 must never send pregnancy information to AdMob as advertising targeting parameters.

Examples of prohibited custom advertising signals include:

pregnancy_week=22

due_date=2027-01-01

trimester=2

pregnant=true

medical_note=...

symptom=...

Pregnancy-related information and the advertising layer should remain technically separated.

---

# Development Ads

Developers must use Google test ads during development.

Developers and testers must not intentionally click production advertisements.

Artificial impressions, clicks, automated traffic, incentivised clicks, click exchanges, or other invalid traffic must not be generated.

---

# Data Deletion

Dad40 provides users with the ability to delete locally stored Dad40 data.

This may include:

- due date
- optional name
- notes
- milestones
- checklist information
- mission completion
- notification preferences
- baby-arrival information

Dad40 cannot independently delete information processed by Google under Google's own systems and policies.
