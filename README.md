This Privacy Policy explains how **BrainRush — Offline Mini Puzzle Arcade** ("BrainRush," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Android application.

BrainRush is an offline, single-player puzzle game designed for a general audience aged **13 and older**. The App includes mini-games such as Mental Math, Number Sequence, Memory Grid, Odd One Out, Word Scramble, Quick Reaction, Schulte Table, Logic Match, and a Daily Challenge.

BrainRush does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

Although BrainRush's gameplay data is stored locally on your device, the App uses **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK** to display advertisements and **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK** to manage advertising consent and privacy choices. These Google services may process certain device and advertising information as described below.

By using BrainRush, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 3. Device Permissions

BrainRush is designed to request only permissions reasonably related to its features.

### INTERNET

**Permission:** `android.permission.INTERNET`

Used to:

* Load Google AdMob advertisements;
* Communicate with advertising providers; and
* Display Google UMP consent and privacy messages.

Core puzzle gameplay does not require an internet connection.

### ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

**Permission:** `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`

Used to determine whether a network connection is available before attempting to load advertising-related content.

### VIBRATE

**Permission:** `android.permission.VIBRATE`

Used to provide optional tactile/haptic feedback during gameplay.

Haptic feedback may be disabled through the App's settings or applicable device settings.

### POST_NOTIFICATIONS

**Permission:** `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`

On supported Android versions, BrainRush may request permission to display an optional local notification reminding you about the Daily Challenge.

Daily Challenge reminders are scheduled locally using Android system functionality such as AlarmManager.

You may deny or revoke notification permission at any time through your Android device settings.

### Permissions We Do Not Request

BrainRush does not require access to:

* Camera;
* Microphone;
* Precise GPS location;
* Contacts;
* Call logs;
* SMS messages;
* Photos;
* Videos;
* General device file storage; or
* Other comparable sensitive device resources unrelated to the App.
